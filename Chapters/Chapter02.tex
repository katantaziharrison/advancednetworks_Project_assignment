\chapter{Research Question}
\label{ch:research-question}

The project aims to examine how proposed 6G capabilities
could affect cybersecurity practice through a comparison
with established 5G security mechanisms.

The main research question is:

\begin{quote}
\emph{How might proposed 6G capabilities change the way
cybersecurity teams secure, test, and monitor wireless
networks compared with 5G?}
\end{quote}

The investigation will address this question by identifying
the relevant 5G protections, examining security concerns
documented for the three selected capability areas,
and assessing the potential implications for practitioner
tasks.

This question is feasible within the project period
because it can be investigated through a bounded
analysis of published sources. The conclusions will
concern what the selected literature supports and
what remains uncertain about future implementation.
